# V47 — Replacement options visible immediately

The Annoyed.zip replacement options were present in V46, but they were rendered below the full body-part grid, so on a long character-part list they could be far down the right panel.

## Changed
- Replacement options now appear **immediately below the selected part card**.
- When Head / Face is selected, the Annoyed.zip Head replacement is shown at the top.
- When a hand/arm is selected, the Annoyed.zip hand options are shown at the top.
- When a body/torso part is selected, the Annoyed.zip body/torso option is shown at the top.
- Applied replacement is clearly marked with **✓ Applied**.
- Manual **Upload Own** and **Clear** controls are also directly underneath.
- The full **All Character Parts** grid is now moved below the replacement controls.

This removes the need to scroll through every body-part thumbnail before reaching the replacement library.
